# CMT – Unit support

Unit support is the part of the Support function that composes the units – leader teams and scouts – and follows each of them. It is also the contact point a member reaches when they do not know who owns their question.

## Why they use Campfire

- **Following a unit.** Opening a unit shows its leaders and its scouts together, each with a way to reach them.
- **Answering a member.** A search across the whole list of participants finds the person who asked and their unit, and the unit opens to their leaders.

The role `wsj27:cmt:support:avdelningssupport` marks the function. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
