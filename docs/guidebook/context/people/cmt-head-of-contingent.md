# CMT – Head of Contingent

The heads of contingent – HoC – lead the contingent and answer for it, and the CMT's functions report to them. They lead rather than staff: a head of contingent works with no unit and no IST job of their own.

## Why they use Campfire

- **The whole contingent at a glance.** The groups view lists every unit, every IST patrol, and every CMT function, with how many people each holds.
- **Finding anyone.** A search across the whole list of participants reaches any member and their contact persons.

A role under `wsj27:cmt:hoc` marks the function. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
