# CMT – Communication

Communication runs the contingent's website and social media, and keeps every member informed before, during, and after the jamboree.

## Why they use Campfire

- **Reaching the right people.** Narrowing the list of participants to leaders, IST, or one unit, then mailing everyone in it or their contact persons, or copying the addresses into another tool.
- **Answering someone who wrote in.** A search finds the person, their unit, and how to reach them.

A role under `wsj27:cmt:kommunikation` marks the function. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
