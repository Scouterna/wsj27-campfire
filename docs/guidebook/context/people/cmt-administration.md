# CMT – Administration

Administration is the contingent management team's function for finances, legal matters, and the contingent's records. It keeps the registrations, invoicing, and paperwork behind every member of the contingent straight.

## Why they use Campfire

- **Finding one person fast.** A search across the whole list of participants by name, unit, or CMT function says whether someone is in the contingent, where they belong, and how to reach them and their contact persons.
- **Reaching a group.** Narrowing the list to a participation role, or opening one unit, gives the people behind a question, with their addresses ready to mail or copy.

A role under `wsj27:cmt:admin` marks the function. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
