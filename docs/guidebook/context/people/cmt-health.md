# CMT – Health

Health is the part of the Support function that looks after the contingent's health, care, and welfare. It helps the units and the IST with health questions, and prepares for the medication, allergies, and conditions a camp of teenagers abroad has to handle.

## Why they use Campfire

- **Looking up a person's health.** A participant's page carries their diet and allergies, vaccinations, medication and how it is kept, medical conditions, equipment, and support needs – what a clinic, a kitchen, or a leader has to be told.
- **Reaching the people around them.** The same page has the contact persons to call when a scout is taken ill.
- **Keeping cases.** A reaction, a fever, medication to follow up – a case about the person holds the team's notes in one place and stays open until it is done, instead of living in a chat thread that scrolls away. The cases about a person are one step from their page.

The role `wsj27:cmt:support:halsa` marks the function and is one of the two health grants – the other is a personal grant set on the registration form. A holder reads the health answers across the whole contingent – the [participants service](../systems/participants-service) refuses them to every other CMT role – and is offered the cases section, which no other role sees. A unit's own leaders read their scouts' answers without a grant.
