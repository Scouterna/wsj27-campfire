# CMT – Support

Support is the CMT function for applications and the contingent members' contact point. Most of its members serve in one of its three parts – [Health](./cmt-health), [IST support](./cmt-ist-support), and [Unit support](./cmt-unit-support) – and this audience is the rest: the function's funktionsansvariga, who answer for all of it, and Specialsupport.

## Why they use Campfire

- **The function as a whole.** The groups view lists Support beside each of its parts, with how many people each holds.
- **Finding anyone.** A search across the whole list of participants reaches any member, the unit or IST patrol they belong to, and their contact persons.

A role under `wsj27:cmt:support` that names none of the parts marks the function – `fa` and `fa-cet` for the funktionsansvariga, and `specialsupport`. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
