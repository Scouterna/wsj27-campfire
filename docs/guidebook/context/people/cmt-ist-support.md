# CMT – IST support

IST support is the part of the Support function that selects and follows the contingent's International Service Team – the adult volunteers whose jobs at the jamboree the host assigns. An IST member belongs to no unit, so their line back to the contingent runs through this function.

## Why they use Campfire

- **Seeing the IST as one group.** The unit browser has an entry for the IST beside the units, and the list of participants narrows to them, so everyone the function follows is in one place.
- **Reaching them.** Mailing every IST member at once, or finding one by name.

The role `wsj27:cmt:support:ist-support` marks the function. Like every CMT role it reads the whole list of participants, but not the health answers, which need the [health grant](./cmt-health).
