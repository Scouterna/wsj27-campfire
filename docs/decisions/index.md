# Decisions

Campfire's architectural decisions are recorded as Architecture Decision Records under `docs/decisions` – one file per decision, holding the context, the choice, and the trade-offs, so the reasoning survives long after the choice was made. Follow a row to read one in full. The first thirty-one were accepted together on 2026-09-15, when the skeleton was first pushed, and they are numbered in reading order rather than in the order they were drafted.
