## Context

The situation and the forces at play – what problem is being solved, and which constraints, requirements, and trade-offs bear on it. Write enough that someone reading this in two years understands why a decision was needed at all.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly and in active voice: "We will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Name the trade-offs being accepted, not only the upside – including anything the team now has to live with or revisit later.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were weighed and why they were not chosen. One short paragraph or bullet for each.
