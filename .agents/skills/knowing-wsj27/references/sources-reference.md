# Sources Reference

Where this skill's facts come from, when they were last checked, where sources disagree, and how to update the skill.

## Last checked

- Sources last checked 25 September 2026, for version 2.0.
- The newest Bulletin is Bulletin 4, July 2026. Bulletin 5 is expected around February 2027.

## Sources

- **jamboree2027.org** and its subpages – the official site: dates, venue, theme, program, eligibility, organizers, and the resident-facing page at /mieszkancy.
- **The official Bulletins** – Bulletins 1–4. Current documents now live on HoC Base at knowledge.wsj2027.pl, which needs a Head of Contingent login, so newer material has to come from a Head of Contingent.
- **scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen** and its subpages – the Swedish contingent: /om-wsj, /deltagare, /ledare, /ist, and the Världsläget page.

## Discrepancies

- **Total attendance.** The site has said "about 176 countries" and "up to 50,000 people". Its resident page now says more than 170 countries and territories, and about 42,000 participants, leaders, and volunteers plus 8,000 guests and day visitors. Treat any total as provisional and cite its source.
- **IST arrival.** Bulletin 4 says 27 July; the Jamboree Dates page still says 27–28 July. Prefer the Bulletin.
- **IST departure.** Bulletin 4, the Jamboree Dates page, and the Swedish IST timeline say 10 August; the Swedish IST page's introduction says 11 August. The introduction is most likely the error.
- **The opening.** One Bulletin 3 schedule grid shows arrival 30 July and opening 31 July. The Terms and Conditions – arrival 29 July, opening 30 July – are authoritative.

## Settled

- There are four hubs – Yellow, Red, Green, and Purple – with four subcamps each. A paragraph copied between Bulletins 3 and 4 says "five colours", and is wrong.
- The ordinal is 26th. Some early material said 27th.

## Updating the skill

1. Re-crawl jamboree2027.org and scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen, and read any Bulletin newer than the one above.
2. Compare against the reference files. Keep official and Swedish facts apart, and keep the skill at the level someone building for the contingent needs – no fee tables, payment schedules, or named individuals.
3. Update the changed facts and the discrepancies here, and refresh Last checked.
4. Bump `metadata.version` in `SKILL.md` – minor for a refresh, major for a restructuring.
