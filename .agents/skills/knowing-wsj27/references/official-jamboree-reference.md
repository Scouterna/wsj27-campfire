## Schedule

- IST arrive 27 July 2027 and leave 10 August. The Jamboree Dates page still says 27–28 July for arrival; Bulletin 4 says 27 July.
- Participants arrive in patrols on 29 July and leave on 9 August.
- The Opening Game and Opening Ceremony are 30 July, the Modular Programme runs 31 July – 8 August, 1 August celebrates 120 years of Scouting, and the Closing Ceremony is 8 August.

## The site

- Wyspa Sobieszewska is an island with ecological-reserve status inside Gdansk, about 20 minutes from the old town.
- Four hubs – Yellow, Red, Green, Purple – each hold four subcamps, around 2,250 participants in about 56 units per subcamp.
- The Plaza is the central marketplace, with the World Scout Centre and the contingent pavilions, open to participants, IST, and day visitors.
- Drinking water, toilets, and showers are on municipal supply. Unit areas have no mains power; powerbanks charge free at service areas. Wi-Fi covers large areas but not the whole site, and temporary cell towers add coverage.
- The weather in late July averages 17 °C with highs around 21 °C, and nights can fall to about 5 °C.

## The program

Three components run under the "Bravely" theme:

- **The Modular Programme** – the core themed activities, 31 July – 8 August, rotated so every subcamp completes every module
- **The Subcamp Programme** – daily subcamp life, the Opening Game, Cultural Day, and the Solidarity Badge
- **The Plaza Programme** – walk-in cultural activities with no registration

Bulletin 4 adds a Pre-Jamboree Task units prepare before arriving.

## The IST

The International Service Team are the adult volunteers who run the event. The host, not the contingent, assigns each to a job – program, safety and medical, site operations, food, communications, and more. IST choose jobs from a catalogue until 1 February 2027, learn their allocation in April 2027, and train online and then on site. They eat in central dining halls rather than cooking in units.

## Food

Participants cook for themselves. Each unit collects ingredients for three meals a day from its hub's food market and cooks in its own area, with shared unit cooking equipment supplied.

## Arrival and insurance

- The official entry points are Gdansk airport, Gdansk Główny station, the Gdansk and Gdynia ferry terminals, and the bus park at AmberExpo.
- Everyone checks in at the Check-in Centre at AmberExpo before entering the site. Youth arrive only in their patrols.
- Medical and travel insurance for the whole stay is mandatory. On-site care is first aid and pre-hospital care only.
- The jamboree is alcohol-free, and Polish law applies.
