# Swedish WSJ27 Costs and Registration Reference

This reference gives the Swedish contingent's fees, payment deadlines, eligibility cutoffs, background-check requirements, and registration/refund terms for WSJ27. Figures come from Scouterna's "Ansöknings- och anmälningsvillkor" (Application and Registration Terms and Conditions) PDF, at media.scoutcontent.se/uploads/2025/11/Ansokningsvillkor-WSJ-2027.pdf. All amounts are in Swedish kronor (SEK, "kr") and are invoiced by Scouterna in staged installments. These Swedish member fees are separate from the official NSO fees in PLN (see `contingent-structure-reference.md`). For structure and travel options in context, see `swedish-contingent-guide.md`; for host-side eligibility, see `jamboree2027-official-reference.md`.

## Eligibility by Role

From the application terms PDF:

- Participants (Deltagare), ages 14–17: born earliest 31 July 2009 and latest 30 July 2013; must be a member of Scouterna or a partner/cooperating organization (samverkansorganisation) at application and throughout participation. (This matches the official rule exactly – "born after 30 July 2009" in `jamboree2027-official-reference.md` means born 31 July 2009 at the earliest, and both sources close at 30 July 2013. The two are the same window, worded differently.)
- Unit Leaders (Avdelningsledare): at least 18 years old by 30 April 2026, and a member of Scouterna or a partner organization.
- IST (International Service Team): must have turned 18 by 30 July 2027 at the latest; member of Scouterna or a partner organization throughout. If under 18 at time of application, guardian approval is required. The Baltic round-trip option is offered ONLY to IST members born 31 July 2001 or later.
- CMT (Kontingentledning): at least 18 years old by 30 April 2026, and a member of Scouterna or a partner organization.

## Fees by Role and Travel Option

All fees are in SEK and invoiced by Scouterna in installments. Totals below are stated in the terms document.

Participants (Deltagare):

- With round trip via the Baltics: application fee 3,500 + 10,500 (30 Jun 2026) + 10,500 (1 Nov 2026) + 10,000 (1 Feb 2027) = 34,500 total.
- With direct travel to Poland: application fee 3,500 + 7,000 + 7,000 + 7,000 = 24,500 total.

Unit Leaders (Avdelningsledare):

- With round trip via the Baltics: registration fee 3,500 + 7,000 + 7,000 + 7,000 = 24,500 total.
- With direct travel to Poland: registration fee 3,500 + 3,700 (30 Jun 2026) + 3,700 (1 Nov 2026) + 3,600 (1 Feb 2027) = 14,500 total.

IST (International Service Team):

- With round trip via the Baltics: application fee 3,500 + 8,700 + 8,700 + 8,600 = 29,500 total.
- With own travel to Poland: application fee 3,500 + 5,500 + 5,500 + 5,000 = 19,500 total.

CMT (Kontingentledning):

- Invoiced in two installments: 5,000 (1 Nov 2026) + 5,000 (1 Feb 2027) = 10,000 total.

What the fee includes: a shared travel insurance is included in the event fee (coverage, deductible, and a contact person to be published before departure). Travel to and from Sweden is included in both participant travel options.

## Payment Schedule

Installment due dates (from the terms PDF):

- Application/registration fee: at time of application (the application counts as complete only once this fee is paid).
- 30 June 2026: second installment (all roles except CMT).
- 1 November 2026: third installment (and first CMT installment).
- 1 February 2027: final installment.

Late/unpaid fees: if payment is not received on time, the contingent spot may be lost; a reminder is sent by email. No interest or reminder fees apply, but unpaid fees can be sent to debt collection (inkasso).

## Application Process

- Participants: the guardian (vårdnadshavare) submits the completed application form digitally via Scoutnet and pays the application fee. The application becomes binding after the final application date.
- Unit Leaders: after being admitted and offered a leader position, registration (anmälan) is done via Scoutnet; complete only once the registration fee is paid, and binding once confirmed in Scoutnet.
- IST: applies digitally via Scoutnet; complete only once the application fee is paid; binding after the final application date.
- Travel option assignment: if a participant cannot be admitted to their chosen travel option due to limited capacity, they may be offered the other option.

## Background Checks and Requirements

Applies to leaders, IST, and CMT (all adult roles):

- A criminal-record extract (utdrag ur belastningsregistret) no more than 1 year old.
- An approved Trygga Möten (Safe Meetings) certificate no more than 3 years old.

General participant requirements:

- At application, the applicant (or guardian, for minors) affirms the applicant is physically and mentally capable of completing the event. If false information leads to a safety problem, Scouterna may deny participation without refund.
- Each participant is responsible for a valid passport/travel document and any vaccination or travel requirements; no refund is given if participation is prevented by missing documents.
- All participants must remain members of Scouterna or a partner organization throughout and follow Scouterna's policies and core values.

## Refunds and Cancellation

Anyone who has applied/registered must pay the full event fee, with these exceptions:

- Withdrawal before the final application date: no fee charged; already-paid application fee refunded.
- Not admitted: application fee and any other payments refunded.
- Event canceled or aborted: fee refunded minus costs Scouterna has already incurred or committed to for the project.
- If Scouterna advances costs for medical care, medication, or an unplanned return trip not covered by the shared insurance, those costs are invoiced to the affected person (or their guardian if under 18).
