# Swedish WSJ27 Contingent Guide

This guide explains how the Swedish Scout and Guide Association (Scouterna) participates in the World Scout Jamboree 2027 (WSJ27), based on scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen and its subpages. It covers the contingent's size and structure, the four participation roles, travel options, the CMT leadership, the preparation timeline, and contact channels. For host-side event facts (dates, venue, eligibility, program), see `jamboree2027-official-reference.md`; for detailed fees, eligibility cutoffs, and registration terms, see `costs-and-registration-reference.md`.

## Contingent Overview

Scouterna organizes and recruits the Swedish national contingent to WSJ27 (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen). Key facts:

- The Swedish contingent is planned at 2,631 people – described as the largest number of scouts Scouterna has ever sent to a World Scout Jamboree.
- The jamboree is hosted in Poland on Wyspa Sobieszewska, on the Baltic coast just outside Gdansk.
- Scouterna frames a "camp period" (lagerperiod) of 29 July – 9 August 2027, with the Opening Ceremony on 30 July 2027 (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/deltagare). This span differs from the official core dates of 30 July – 8 August 2027 (see `jamboree2027-official-reference.md`) because the Swedish framing includes contingent arrival and departure days.
- All Swedish scouts attend as a single unified national contingent within the jamboree, which is logistically run on-site by the Polish host organization (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/cmt-lagerledningen).

## Participation Roles

Swedes take part in four roles (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen and subpages):

- Deltagare (Participants): scouts aged 14–17 at the Opening Ceremony (30 July 2027).
- Ledare (Unit Leaders): adults responsible for a unit of scouts.
- IST (International Service Team): adult volunteers (staff/functionaries), minimum age 18. IST help with practical tasks – running a program activity, cooking, transporting visitors, filming/photographing the camp. Tasks worldwide are planned and assigned by the Polish Scout Federation, not by Sweden.
- CMT (Contingent Management Team): the Swedish leadership team (see below).

A Swedish-run program function, "Swedish Foodhouse," operates a camp restaurant serving Swedish food and running activities for international participants; it recruits its own volunteers (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/ist/foodhouse).

## Contingent Structure

From the leader page (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/ledare):

- The contingent totals 2,631 people across 53 units (avdelningar).
- Each unit consists of 36 scouts plus 4 leaders, who share responsibility for the scouts together.
- Accepted leaders are assigned a travel code such as "D27" (direct travel) or "R13" (round trip), where D = direktresa (direct) and R = rundresa (round trip). These codes are distinct from the eventual unit numbers.

## Travel Options

Swedish participants and leaders choose between two travel options (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/deltagare, /ledare):

- Round trip (rundresa, "tur och retur"): includes a tour through the Baltic region (Latvia and/or Lithuania) before the camp. Departs Sweden 21–22 July 2027.
- Direct travel (direktresa, "direkt"): travels only to the Gdansk camp. Departs Sweden 26–27 July 2027.
- Return to Sweden: 9–10 August 2027.

For IST, travel options are a round trip through the Baltics (offered only to IST members born 31 July 2001 or later) or independent travel (egen resa), where the IST member books and pays their own travel. Rovers (IST aged 18–25) may join an organized multi-stop tour with activities before the jamboree (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/ist). Fee amounts for each option are in `costs-and-registration-reference.md`.

## The CMT (Contingent Management Team)

The Swedish contingent is led by the CMT, called "lagerledningen" in Swedish (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/cmt-lagerledningen and /om-wsj/cmt-lagerledningen). Note that scouterna.se uses both "Contingent Management Team" and, on the hub page, "Camp Management Team / Lagerarrangörerna" for the same body.

Structure:

- Four functional areas collaborate, led by three Head of Contingent (HOC) chiefs.
- Admin: finances, legal matters, and structural/spreadsheet work.
- Kommunikation (Communication): website, social media, and information before, during, and after camp.
- Support: interviews and reviews WSJ27 applications; primary contact point for contingent members toward CMT.
- Program: builds the program, arranges pre-camp meetings (förträffar), and plans the tour (rundresa) activities.

The CMT recruits volunteers (for example "Operativt HQ och ledningsstöd" and "IT-stöd för admin"), with applications via jobb.scouterna.se.

## Preparation and Timeline

- Acceptance notices: the hub and participant pages state participants and IST are notified before Midsummer ("innan midsommar") 2026, and leaders in spring 2026. The leader page separately says leader acceptance notices are sent out by email during May. Sources are inconsistent on the exact timing and year for leaders; treat leader-notice timing as "spring/May 2026" pending confirmation (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen, /deltagare, /ledare).
- Leaders: two in-person (fysiska) unit meetings are planned during 2026–2027, plus additional digital meetings between them.
- Participants: the deltagare attend two unit förträffar (avdelning meetings) – Förträff 1 in autumn (hösten) 2026, to get to know the unit, prepare, and build a secure group; and Förträff 2 in spring (våren) 2027, a final pre-departure meeting to make the unit ready to travel (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/deltagare). The page does not mark them as mandatory.
- IST prep meetings (förträffar): Förträff 1 preliminarily 4 October (year not stated); Förträff 2 in spring 2027 (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/ist).
- A waitlist (reservlista) exists for those who missed applying; join by contacting the CMT.

## Profile Package and Sustainability

- Profile package (profilpaket): every contingent member receives badges, a backpack, a T-shirt, and more, all bearing the Swedish contingent's WSJ logo (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/om-wsj/profilpaket).
- The package does NOT include a scout shirt (scoutskjorta); for sustainability reasons, participants bring their own or buy/find one (for example scoutshop.se). A sizing chart (3XS to 5XL) is provided for the contingent T-shirt.
- Sustainability: the Swedish delegation follows Scouterna's national sustainability plan, with three jamboree focus areas – Resor (Travel), Material & inköp (Materials and Procurement), and Kunskap & påverkan (Knowledge and Impact) (scouterna.se/k/wsj27-polen/om-wsj/hallbarhet-pa-wsj).
