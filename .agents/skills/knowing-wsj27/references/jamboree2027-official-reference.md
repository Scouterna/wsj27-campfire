## Event Identity and Theme

The event is officially the 26th World Scout Jamboree, held in Poland in 2027 under the theme "Bravely" (also written "Bravely!"). Organizers describe the theme as a call to inspire young people to act bravely for the future of humanity and the planet. It coincides with the 120th anniversary of the Scout Movement.

A naming caution:

- Most official material, including the Bulletins and the Jamboree Dates page, calls it the 26th World Scout Jamboree. The previous-jamborees list ends at the 25th (Republic of Korea, 2023), so 26th is the correct ordinal; the Polish government act likewise uses the Roman numeral XXVI (26).
- The abbreviation "WSJ27" refers to the year 2027, not the ordinal number.

## Dates and Schedule

Core event: 30 July – 8 August 2027 (Opening Ceremony 30 July, Closing Ceremony 8 August). The full published schedule (jamboree2027.org/stories/jamboree-dates, Bulletin 3 p.31):

- IST arrival: 27 July 2027; IST departure: 10 August 2027.
- Participants (patrols) arrival: 29 July 2027; participant departure: 9 August 2027.
- Opening Game and Opening Ceremony: 30 July 2027.
- Modular Programme: 31 July – 8 August 2027.
- 120 Years of Scouting Celebration: 1 August 2027.
- Closing Ceremony: 8 August 2027.

Conflicts to note:

- The Polish resident-facing page (jamboree2027.org/mieszkancy) lists the Opening Ceremony as 31 July 2027 and about 48,000 attendees, differing from the Jamboree Dates page.
- One schedule grid inside Bulletin 3 (p.63) shows participant arrivals on 30 July and a "First Day Quest" plus Opening Ceremony on 31 July, which contradicts the same Bulletin's narrative (arrival 29 July, opening 30 July) and the Terms and Conditions (arrival 29 July). The Terms and Conditions dates (participant arrival 29 July, core event 30 July – 8 August) are the authoritative ones.
- Scouterna frames a "camp period" of 29 July – 9 August 2027, spanning arrival and departure days (see `swedish-contingent-guide.md`).

## Venue and Site Logistics

The venue is Wyspa Sobieszewska (Sobieszewska Island), within the city limits of Gdansk on the Baltic coast in the Pomorskie (Pomeranian) region of northern Poland (jamboree2027.org/stories/jamboree-venue, jamboree2027.org/stories/host-city-gdansk).

Venue facts:

- The island has ecological-reserve status and is about a 20-minute drive from Gdansk's historic center; it was formed by regulating the estuary of the Vistula River.
- The Jamboree site covers about 300 hectares of fields and meadows, with activity areas in the forest and on the beach; the site sits on elevated grassy fields with established drainage.
- July/August climate: warm temperate, average daily temperature 17 degrees C, average high 21 degrees C, humidity around 55 percent, roughly seven days of precipitation per month. Nights can drop to about 5 degrees C. Advice: sunhat, windbreaker, raincoat, and a tent with long pegs for wind.

Site infrastructure (Bulletin 3, pp.42–43):

- Water: the site is connected to municipal water and sewage; marked drinking-water taps are distributed throughout; Gdansk tap water is safe to drink.
- Toilets and showers: modern facilities in individual cubicles, distributed across the site and regularly cleaned.
- Waste: a five-bin segregation system (paper, glass, metal and plastic, bio waste, other), matching Gdansk's municipal system.
- Power: unit areas are not connected to the grid; electricity is available in designated service areas, and powerbanks can be charged free at designated stations.
- Connectivity: free Wi-Fi zones cover large areas (not the whole site); temporary cell towers are added for coverage.

Site layout: four hubs named by color (Yellow, Red, Green, Purple), each hosting four subcamps for 16 participant subcamps total (see `contingent-structure-reference.md`).

## Eligibility and Scale

Eligibility (jamboree2027.org, Bulletin 3 Terms and Conditions Section 2):

- Youth Participants: 14–17 at the start of the event, born after 30 July 2009 and not later than 30 July 2013.
- IST (International Service Team): 18 and above, born on or before 30 July 2009.
- Adults, including Adult Patrol Leaders and Contingent Management Team members, must be 18 and above (born on or before 30 July 2009).

Scale – sources differ, and this is a moving number:

- The website markets the venue as able to welcome up to 50,000 people in total (participants, volunteers, and day visitors) from about 176 countries (jamboree2027.org/jamboree101) – a combined total, not a youth-participant figure. The Heads of Contingent FAQ caps day visitors at up to 1,000 per day.
- Bulletin 4 (July 2026) Terms and Conditions cap a single contingent at 10% of the total approved participants and at no more than 3,600 people – an absolute per-contingent ceiling, not a statement of the total. The host country (Poland) may send up to 20%.
- The Polish resident page cites about 48,000 total attendees.
- Bulletin 4 reaffirms that the World Scout Committee and ZHP are running an in-depth review of Jamboree planning (risk profile and mitigation) under the WOSM Events Strategy, with reviews continuing at regular intervals as the Jamboree approaches and a first report shared with Heads of Contingent in early 2026. Treat total-attendance figures as provisional and cite the source and date.

## Organizing Bodies and Governance

Host: Związek Harcerstwa Polskiego (ZHP, the Polish Scouting and Guiding Association), founded 1918, 110,000+ members, a founding member of WOSM and WAGGGS, motto "Czuwaj!" ("Be Prepared!"). ZHP works with WOSM and the City of Gdansk through the 26th World Scout Jamboree Organising Committee, a Polish foundation. ZHP was entrusted with hosting by the World Scout Conference in 2021.

Governance and named leadership (jamboree2027.org/stories/jamboree-organisation, Bulletin 3 invitations):

- Jamboree Management Team (JMT): highest decision-making body, 12 members.
- Jamboree Planning Team (JPT): 200+ volunteers from 40+ countries across 22 departments.
- Executive Director: Karol Gzyl. Finance Director: Artur Glebko. ZHP Chief Commissioner: Martyna Kowacka.
- WOSM Secretary General: David Berg. World Scout Committee Chairperson: Daniël Corsen.
- Mayor of Gdansk: Aleksandra Dulkiewicz. Marshal of Pomorskie: Mieczysław Struk.
- Honorary Committee chaired by the Prime Minister of Poland; a Government Plenipotentiary for the Jamboree (Tomasz Szymanski) was appointed in March 2025.

## Arrival, Entry Points, and Insurance

Official Entry Points: Gdansk Lech Walesa International Airport (GDN, 12 km from the city center), Gdansk Glowny railway station, Gdansk ferry terminal, Gdynia ferry terminal, and the bus parking at AmberExpo/Polsat Plus Arena.

Arrival (Bulletin 3, pp.100–101):

- All arrivals are shuttled to a Check-in Centre at AmberExpo (Zaglowa 11, Gdansk); no one enters the campsite without completing check-in there.
- Youth participants arrive exclusively in patrols; unaccompanied minors may not check in alone.

Insurance (Bulletin 3 p.112, Terms and Conditions Section 7):

- Valid medical and travel insurance covering the full stay (including travel to and from Poland) is mandatory for all attendees. On-site medical facilities are limited to first aid and pre-hospital care; further treatment is at the participant's own expense and is not covered by the Jamboree fee.
- Schengen-visa applicants must hold travel medical insurance with minimum EUR 30,000 coverage (emergency treatment, hospitalization, evacuation, repatriation), valid across the Schengen area.
- EU/EEA citizens should bring a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) and are still advised to add travel insurance; repatriation is not covered by Jamboree arrangements.

Travel partners: the official carrier is LOT Polish Airlines (group offers, <jamboree@lot.pl>); the official Polish tour operator for pre/post-Jamboree tours is Mazurkas Travel; Eurail/Interrail offer group and individual rail options.
