## The contingent

Scouterna sends one national contingent of 2,631 people, the largest it has sent to a World Scout Jamboree – 53 units of 36 scouts and 4 leaders, plus the IST and the CMT.

## The roles

- **Deltagare** – youth participants, 14–17 at the Opening Ceremony.
- **Ledare** – unit leaders, adults who share responsibility for a unit's scouts, four to a unit.
- **IST** – funktionärer, adult volunteers of 18 or older whose jobs the host assigns. Swedish Foodhouse, a Swedish-run restaurant on site, recruits its own IST.
- **CMT** – the contingent management team, kontingentledningen in Swedish, which leads the contingent. Unlike deltagare, ledare, and IST, it still recruits volunteers as needs arise.

## Units

A unit (avdelning) is 36 scouts and 4 leaders, and its scouts are divided into four patrols (patruller) of nine. The units were announced on 19 September 2026. IST belong to no unit, but will be divided into patrols of their own; how is not decided yet.

## The CMT

Three Heads of Contingent lead four functions:

- **Admin** – finances, legal matters, and the contingent's records
- **Kommunikation** – the website, social media, and information before, during, and after camp
- **Support** – applications, and the contact point for contingent members
- **Program** – the program, the pre-camp meetings, and the round trip's activities

## The trip

- **Round trip (rundresa)** – through Latvia and Lithuania before camp. Deltagare and ledare leave Sweden 21–22 July 2027. IST who are rovers (18–25) on the round trip leave earlier, 19 July.
- **Direct travel (direktresa)** – straight to Gdansk, leaving 26–27 July.
- **Own travel (egen resa)** – IST may travel on their own instead.
- **The camp period** – Scouterna frames it as 29 July – 9 August, arrival to departure.
- **Home** – deltagare and ledare return 9–10 August; IST leave the site 10 August.

## Before the trip

- Deltagare and ledare meet their unit twice: Förträff 1 in autumn 2026 to get to know each other, and Förträff 2 in spring 2027 to get ready to travel. Leaders also meet digitally in between.
- IST have their own förträffar, a digital one in October 2026 and one in Stockholm in early 2027.
- Every member gets a profile package – badges, a backpack, a T-shirt – but not a scout shirt, which members bring themselves.
- The contingent follows Scouterna's sustainability plan, with travel, materials and purchasing, and knowledge and influence as its focus areas.

## The security situation

A page named "Världsläget" sets out how the contingent treats the war in Ukraine: debris from missiles and drones has fallen on Polish territory, and GPS and communications can be disrupted around the Baltic Sea. The CMT keeps in contact with the Swedish Ministry for Foreign Affairs and the embassy in Poland, and states that participants' safety comes before any travel route, and before taking part at all.
