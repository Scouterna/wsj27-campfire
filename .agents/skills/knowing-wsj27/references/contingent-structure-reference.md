# WSJ27 Contingent Structure and Terms Reference

This reference describes how any national contingent is formed and charged at the official level, drawn from the official Bulletins' "How to Form a Contingent" and Terms and Conditions sections (current as of Bulletin 4, July 2026, which reaffirms the Bulletin 3 framework – fees, payment schedule, and contingent limits unchanged). It covers roles, patrol/unit structure, formation rules, CMT sizing, the official NSO fees in PLN, the payment schedule, and Terms and Conditions highlights. This is the WOSM/host-level framework – for Sweden's own roles, fees, and deadlines see `swedish-contingent-guide.md` and `costs-and-registration-reference.md`.

## Roles and Definitions

From the Terms and Conditions, Section 2:

- Youth Participants: aged 14–17 at the start, born after 30 July 2009 and not later than 30 July 2013.
- Adult Patrol Leader (APL): a volunteer over 18 (born on or before 30 July 2009), appointed by the contingent, who leads a patrol of 9 youth and is responsible for them.
- PAX: the collective term for Adult Patrol Leaders and their youth participants.
- International Service Team (IST): adult volunteers (18+), recruited through their contingent and assigned to a job; essential to running the event.
- Contingent Management Team (CMT): adult volunteers (18+) responsible for participant care, finances, communications, and logistics of the contingent.
- Head of Contingent (HOC): leads the contingent and is part of the CMT; cannot serve as an APL or IST. NSOs were asked to appoint their HOC by 1 November 2024 via the HOC Appointment Form.

## Patrol, Unit, and Contingent Structure

- A patrol is 10 people: 9 youth participants plus 1 Adult Patrol Leader.
- A unit is 4 patrols: 36 youth participants plus 4 Adult Patrol Leaders (40 total). If an NSO cannot fill a unit, international units are formed from patrols of different NSOs.
- On site, units live in subcamps grouped into four hubs (Yellow, Red, Green, Purple), 16 subcamps total, ~2,250 participants and ~56 units per subcamp (see `program-and-activities-reference.md`).
- Contingent limits: only NSOs in good standing with WOSM may attend; one contingent per NSO. No contingent may exceed 10% of the total approved participants, and no contingent may exceed 3,600 people (Bulletin 4 – an absolute per-contingent ceiling, not the event total); the host country (Poland) may send up to 20%. No contingent may have more adults (IST plus CMT) than youth participants, except the host NSO.

## CMT Sizing

Minimum one CMT member for contingents up to 18 participants; two for larger. Allowed CMT size by number of participants (Terms and Conditions):

- 1–18: 1 to 2
- 19–36: 2 to 3
- 37–72: 2 to 5
- 73–144: 2 to 8
- 145–288: 2 to 14
- 289–576: 2 to 22
- 577–1,557: 2 to 30
- 1,558–2,304: 2 to 40
- More than 2,305: discuss with the Jamboree Organisation

## Official NSO Fees

Fees are set by the World Scout Committee, discounted by country category (A–D, based on Gross National Income per capita; an NSO confirms its category on WOSM's Treehouse), and payable in Polish Zloty (PLN). These are the NSO-level fees that a contingent pays to the Jamboree Organisation – they are distinct from what Scouterna charges Swedish members (see `costs-and-registration-reference.md`).

Regular fee (Participant incl. APL / IST-CMT), Bulletin 3 pp.113, 125–126:

- Category A: 1,600 / 1,440 PLN
- Category B: 3,200 / 2,880 PLN
- Category C: 4,800 / 4,320 PLN
- Category D: 6,400 / 5,760 PLN

Early Bird fee (Participant / IST-CMT):

- Category A: 1,500 / 1,350 PLN
- Category B: 3,000 / 2,700 PLN
- Category C: 4,500 / 4,050 PLN
- Category D: 6,000 / 5,400 PLN

## Registration and Payment Schedule

Registration is electronic. An NSO submits a Contingent Allocation form; the Jamboree Organisation confirms the allocation and issues binding invoices. Schedule (Bulletin 3 pp.126–127):

- 1 July 2025 – Early Registration allocation request due.
- 1 August 2025 – Early Registration allocation confirmed (invoices issued).
- 1 September 2025 – Early Registration Deposit due (30%).
- 1 November 2025 – Regular Contingent Allocation request due.
- 1 December 2025 – Allocation confirmed (invoices issued).
- 1 February 2026 – Contingent Deposit due (10%; not for those who paid the Early Bird deposit).
- 1 April 2026 – up to 10% allocation adjustment.
- 1 May 2026 – Second payment due (20%).
- 15 May 2026 – personal-data registration opens.
- 1 November 2026 – Third payment due (30%).
- 1 January 2027 – personal-data registration closes.
- 1 April 2027 – Fourth payment due (40%; discounted for Early Registrations).

The deposit (10% of the fee, or 30% for Early Registration) guarantees the registered number and applies to the contingent, not individuals; 10% of the regular fee is non-refundable. Late payments incur a 5% service fee and risk allocation cancellation. Each contingent pays as a single remittance in PLN (not by individuals) to an assigned account.

## Terms and Conditions Highlights

- What the fee covers: campsite fees for the on-site period, the program and program-related transport, three meals a day and drinking water, transport between an official entry/exit point and the site, shared unit equipment (cooking tent and equipment – no personal equipment), on-site first aid and pre-hospital care for illness/accidents (excluding pre-existing conditions; medications, specialist care, and hospitalization are not included), and a participation kit (scarf and badge).
- Not covered: personal camping gear, clinical/hospital treatment, medications, and repatriation – hence mandatory personal insurance (see `jamboree2027-official-reference.md`).
- Cancellation: no fee is refunded if the event is canceled after 1 July 2027. On Force Majeure cancellation, remaining fees are reimbursed after costs are covered. Deposits and late surcharges are never refunded; visa-denied applicants may be refunded with documentation. The organizers hold event-cancellation insurance (not covering travel, equipment, or pre/post activities).
- Conduct: the Jamboree is alcohol-free; Polish law applies (adulthood 18, applying to tobacco, alcohol, and high-caffeine drinks). Breaches can lead to eviction without refund.
- Legal: governed by Polish law; the English text of the Terms and Conditions prevails in disputes. Terms dated 1 August 2024, updated 1 April 2025.

## Solidarity Operation

The Solidarity Operation funds participation from under-resourced NSOs (Category A and B countries), aiming for at least one patrol from each such Member Organization (Bulletin 3 pp.102–104). It covers a patrol of 9 youth + 1 APL + 1 additional adult (registered as a CMT member), including registration fees, transport, visa fees, and sleeping tents/bags/mats. EUR 10,000 fully funds one patrol; support is via donations, self-run fundraising (4fund.com/pl/jamboree), or marked Scout Shop products. Contact: <solidarity@wsj2027.pl>.
