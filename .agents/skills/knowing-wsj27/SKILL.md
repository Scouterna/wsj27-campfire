# Knowing WSJ27

The World Scout Jamboree 2027 and the Swedish contingent to it, at the level someone building for the contingent needs. WSJ27 is the 26th World Scout Jamboree, hosted by the Polish association ZHP with WOSM. Scouterna recruits and runs the Swedish contingent. The skill keeps official-jamboree facts (jamboree2027.org and the Bulletins) apart from Swedish-contingent facts (scouterna.se), because the two disagree in places.

## When to Use

- Building or writing anything that names the jamboree or the contingent – dates, roles, units, travel, the CMT
- Answering a question about WSJ27 itself: dates, venue, theme, eligibility, hubs and subcamps, the organizers
- Answering a question about the Swedish contingent: its size and units, the roles, travel options, pre-camp meetings, channels
- Deciding whether a fact is the host's or Scouterna's, or which of two conflicting sources to trust

## Key Context

- **The event:** the 26th World Scout Jamboree, theme "Bravely". "WSJ27" is the year, not the ordinal. Opening Ceremony 30 July, Closing Ceremony 8 August 2027.
- **The venue:** Wyspa Sobieszewska, an island within Gdansk on Poland's Baltic coast – about 300 hectares of fields, forest, and beach.
- **The site:** four hubs (Yellow, Red, Green, Purple) of four subcamps each, 16 in total. A patrol is 9 youth and 1 adult patrol leader; a unit is 4 patrols, 36 youth and 4 leaders.
- **Eligibility:** youth are 14–17, born after 30 July 2009 and no later than 30 July 2013. Every adult role is 18 or older.
- **The Swedish contingent:** 2,631 people – 53 units (avdelningar) of 40, plus the IST and the CMT – the largest Sweden has sent to a World Scout Jamboree.
- **The roles:** deltagare (youth participants), ledare (unit leaders), IST (International Service Team, adult volunteers the host assigns to jobs), and the CMT (the Swedish contingent management team, kontingentledningen).
- **The trip:** a round trip (rundresa) through the Baltic states leaving Sweden 21–22 July 2027, or direct travel (direktresa) leaving 26–27 July. Participants arrive on site 29 July and leave 9 August, and are back in Sweden by 10 August; IST leave the site on 10 August.
- **Channels:** each unit uses Discord as its main day-to-day channel. Formal matters – applications, acceptance, invoices – run through Scouterna and Scoutnet.
- **Applications are closed.** Deltagare, ledare, and IST were accepted and assigned to units during 2026, and the reserve list is closed.
