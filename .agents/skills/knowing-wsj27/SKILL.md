# Knowing WSJ27

This skill is reference material about the World Scout Jamboree 2027 (WSJ27) and how the Swedish Scout contingent participates. WSJ27 is the 26th World Scout Jamboree, hosted in Poland by the World Organization of the Scout Movement (WOSM) and the Polish Scouting and Guiding Association (Związek Harcerstwa Polskiego, ZHP). It runs 30 July – 8 August 2027 on Wyspa Sobieszewska in Gdansk, under the theme "Bravely". Scouterna, the Swedish Scout and Guide Association, organizes and recruits Sweden's national contingent – planned as the largest Sweden has ever sent.

Use the reference files below for detail. This skill separates official-jamboree facts (from jamboree2027.org and the official Bulletins) from Swedish-contingent facts (from scouterna.se), and flags where sources disagree.

## Key Context

- Event: the 26th World Scout Jamboree, referred to as WSJ27 or "Poland 2027"; theme "Bravely". "WSJ27" is the year 2027, not the ordinal number.
- Core dates: 30 July – 8 August 2027; Opening Ceremony 30 July, Closing Ceremony 8 August. Participants arrive 29 July and depart 9 August; IST arrive 27 July and depart 10 August.
- Venue: Wyspa Sobieszewska (Sobieszewska Island), an ecological-reserve island within Gdansk on Poland's Baltic coast; a ~300-hectare site.
- Host and governance: ZHP and WOSM, via the 26th World Scout Jamboree Organising Committee (a Polish foundation, KRS 0001022285).
- Youth eligibility: 14–17 at the event, born after 30 July 2009 and not later than 30 July 2013. IST and other adults must be 18+ (born on or before 30 July 2009).
- Scale: the website markets the venue as able to welcome up to 50,000 people in total – participants, volunteers, and day visitors – from about 176 countries. Bulletin 4 (July 2026) caps any single contingent at 10% of the total participants and no more than 3,600 people (a per-contingent ceiling, not the total). A WOSM/host planning review is underway – see `references/jamboree2027-official-reference.md`.
- Site layout: four hubs (Yellow, Red, Green, Purple), 16 participant subcamps (~2,250 each); a unit is 4 patrols (36 youth + 4 leaders = 40); a patrol is 9 youth + 1 Adult Patrol Leader.
- Swedish contingent: planned at 2,631 people across 53 units – the largest Swedish WSJ contingent ever; roles are participants (deltagare), unit leaders (ledare), IST, and CMT (lagerledningen); contact <wsj@scouterna.se>.
- The Jamboree is alcohol-free; valid medical/travel insurance is mandatory for all attendees (Schengen visas require minimum EUR 30,000 medical coverage).
